**一、海外工厂及KD领域业务概述**
«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»车企在海外的制造、生产业务工厂包含两种类型。一种是车企«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei";color:#FA0000'»直接在海外建厂«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»，生产自己研发的车型。为了方便起见，我们把这种类型的工厂为«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei";color:#FA0000'»海外工厂«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»。另一种是海外的厂商针对车企的车型进行«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei";color:#FA0000'»组装、销售«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»，这种类型的工厂称之为«/span»<span style='font-family: Calibri;color:#FA0000'>KD</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"; color:#FA0000'»工厂«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»。«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri'>KD</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»是英文«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri;color:#FA0000'>KnockedDown</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»的缩写，是«/span»<span style='font-family: Calibri'>“</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»散件装配«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri'>”</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»的意思。在汽车行业，按目前国际通行说法，«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri'>KD</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»又分«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri'>CKD</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»、«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri'>SKD</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»，在国际汽车贸易中，整车出口国的汽车公司«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei";color:#FA0000'»把整车予以拆散，以半成品或零部件的方式出口«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»，再由«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"; color:#FA0000'»进口国厂商«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»在所在国以«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei";color:#FA0000'»自行装配«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»方式完成整车制造并进行销售。«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri'>CKD</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»即«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri'>Completely Knocked Down</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»，是«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri'>“</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»全散件组装«/span»<span style='font-family: Calibri'>”</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»的意思，是指进口或引进汽车时，以完全拆散的状态进入，之后再把全部零部件组装成整车。«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri'>SKD</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»即«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri'>Semi-Knocked Down</span>«span style='font-family: "Microsoft YaHei"'»，是«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri'>“</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»半散件组装«/span»<span style='font-family: Calibri'>”</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»的意思，是指从国外进口汽车总成，如发动机、驾驶室、底盘等，然后在本国国内装配制造成整车。«/span»
«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»一般而言，从制造生产管理来看，海外工厂等同于车企的«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei";color:#FA0000'»异地工厂«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»，因此整个整车生产的工艺、物流、生产方面的管理都属于整车厂需要管理的范畴，且海外工厂从信息化的角度，也会遵守车企的国内标准和规范，使用同样的系统。而对于«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri'>KD</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»工厂则相对简单，在海外组装的过程并不需要整车厂负责；整车厂只需要派出«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei";color:#FA0000'»工艺人员«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»进行一定的«/span»«span style='font-family: "Microsoft YaHei";color:#FA0000'»技术支持«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»即可。«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri;color:#FA0000'>KD</span>«span style='font-family: "Microsoft YaHei";color:#FA0000'»组装厂«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»也不需要«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei";color:#FA0000'»用整车厂的«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri;color:#FA0000'>IT</span>«span style='font-family: "Microsoft YaHei";color:#FA0000'»系统«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»，它是一种«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei";color:#FA0000'»相对独立«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»的运作模式。«/span»
KD业务就是针对出口的打散零部件的包装、物流业务。在整车厂，KD业务也是以<span style='color:#FA0000'>项目方式</span>运作的，项目一般分为谈判准备阶段、<span style='color:#FA0000'>物流包装</span>方案规划阶段、<span style='color:#FA0000'>样件试制与发运</span>阶段、小批量生产阶段、批量生产阶段等几个阶段。
但无论是海外工厂还是KD工厂，都会涉及KD业务。KD工厂自不待言；对于海外工厂，整车上的大部分零部件也是从国内采购、制造，然后运输到海外的。一般海外工厂KD打散程度会比纯粹KD工厂更深。需要<span style='color:#FA0000'>出口的打散件清单</span>称为<span style='color:#FA0000'>KD BOM</span>。KD BOM是为海外KD项目服务、反映KD车型海外装配状态和零部件结构的装配清单，同时可基于KD BOM定义供应KD物料的物流、供应需求量等信息。
«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»由KD BOM导致的问题往往比一般的BOM问题带来的损失更大。比如，零部件已经装入集装箱时如果发现某个零部件状态不对，为了找出这个零件，或许需要将所有集装箱打开检查，再«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei";color:#FA0000'»重新装箱«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»。如果零部件已经运到海外工厂，在组装时发现错件，除了要«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei";color:#FA0000'»支付高昂的零部件运输成本«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»之外，还需要承担出口国厂商的索赔费用。相比国内工厂的制造«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri'>BOM</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»，«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri'>KD BOM</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»管理准确度以及精细度要求更高，比如对于«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei";color:#FA0000'»标准件«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»，«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"; color:#FA0000'»国内工厂«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»往往不是靠«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri'>BOM</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»来指导装车，因此«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei";color:#FA0000'»允许«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri;color:#FA0000'>BOM</span>«span style='font-family: "Microsoft YaHei";color:#FA0000'»上不针对标准件进行精细管理«/span»«span style='font-family: "Microsoft YaHei"'»。但对«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri'>KD</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»则不行，因为在海外装车时不可能像国内一样从一大堆标准件中选择、装车，而必须准确。这就要求从发运到装车，«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei";color:#FA0000'»标准件都必须与车型匹配好«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»。部分车企在«/span»<span style='font-family: Calibri'>EBOM</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»上对标准件进行«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei";color:#FA0000'»粗放式管理«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»（如不按照功能区域码«/span»<span style='font-family: Calibri'>+</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»功能位置码的方式管理标准件）«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri'>​</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»，在«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri;color:#FA0000'>KD</span>«span style='font-family: "Microsoft YaHei";color:#FA0000'»业务«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»中则不得不针对«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei";color:#FA0000'»标准件逐个重新核对«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»，不但费时费力，而且工作的成果仅仅局限在单个的«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri'>KD</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»业务中，是一种低效率、被动的工作方式。«/span»
**二、KD BOM管理**
«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»一般车企«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri'>KD</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»相关的业务由«/span»«span style='font-family: "Microsoft YaHei";color:#FA0000'»海外事业部«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»负责，专门负责海外业务的«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei";color:#FA0000'»物流部门«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»将编制«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri'>KD BOM</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»。«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri'>KD BOM</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»的编制主要基于研发发布的«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri;color:#FA0000'>EBOM</span>«span style='font-family: "Microsoft YaHei";color:#FA0000'»以及«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri; color:#FA0000'>KD</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei";color:#FA0000'»项目信息«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»，在此基础上形成初始«/span»<span style='font-family: Calibri;color:#FA0000'>KD BOM</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»，定义«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri;color:#FA0000'>KD</span>«span style='font-family: "Microsoft YaHei";color:#FA0000'»专用件、属地化件«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»（属地化件是指采用出口国供应商的零部件）«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri'>​</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»、«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei";color:#FA0000'»维护路线信息«/span»«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»（包括物流路线）以及供应商信息等。海外事业部物流部门发布维护完成的«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri'>BOM</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»，以支持«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri'>KD</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»项目的包装方案设计以及物流发运等业务。以下主要就«/span»<span style='font-family:Calibri'>KD BOM</span>«span style='font-family:"Microsoft YaHei"'»的构建策略以及主要管理内容进行说明。«/span»

同P/MBOM一样，与EBOM的一体化也是构建KDBOM一个非常重要的要求。EBOM零部件及相关信息要能够自动、无遗漏地传递到KD BOM上，包括零部件的配置条件。发生在EBOM上的工程变更能够自动、无遗漏地传递到KD BOM上。KD BOM通过KD变更单进行变更控制和发布。
**（二）KDBOM管理的内容**
KDBOM管理的内容包括图5-20所示的几个方面。
1）KD件：对于同一零部件，国内、国外供货级别可能不同，如国内生产在总成级别供货，而海外需要子件级别供货，则在车型研发阶段，KD BOM工程师提 出KD拆分需求，在研发阶段根据KD需求对总成件进行打散。
2）属地化件：部分零部件由在海外/KD工厂所在地供应商供货，这部分零部件需要在KDBOM上标识出来。从KD BOM上应该能够导出属地化零部件清单，以支持这些零部件的采购等业务。
3）KD合件：对于需要组装成合件进行<span style='color:#FA0000'>发运的零部件</span>，需要在KD BOM上<span style='color:#FA0000'>体现合件号</span>，并管理其下层零部件。
4）KD件供应商信息：KD零部件供应商某些情况下会与国内生产工厂有差异，除了上面所说的属地化件之外，其他件在国内可能是多个供应商供货，到海外为了简化管理，通常<span style='color:#FA0000'>锁定其中一家</span>。并且，如果是自制件，对于海外工厂/KD工厂而言，则整车厂本身是这些件的供应商。
![image3](76699eca85314d2b8f6adc7eac0f26d8.png)
5）KD工艺信息：主要指路线信息，包括制造路线、装配路线和物流路线等。
6）变更、断点及生效信息：与制造BOM一样，时间轴的管理对于KD BOM也非常重要。KD变更单一方面承接EBOM上发生的工程变更单信息，一方面管理KD内部的变化，并驱动KD BOM状态以及时间生效性管理。从断点层面来看，KD件的切换时间不见得与国内工厂制造BOM上的切换时间同步，但一般需要以此为依据进行切换管理。
