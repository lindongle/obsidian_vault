TC030750 Design BOM Alignment

TC030781 Teamcenter BOM Unit Management,
Prerequisites
TC10101（作者） AND TC010231（变更管理）
Teamcenter BOM Unit Management is for authoring and efficiently managing Unit Engineering Bill of Material for complex Product Assets whose Bill of Materials are configured and tracked by End Item Unit Effectivity. Typical industries that use this style of Engineering BOM are A&D, Transportation and Marine. Teamcenter BOM Unit Management is interoperable across the Teamcenter ecosystem, delivering process efficiency and scalability to support the development and sustainment of complex products throughout their lifecycle. Highlights of the product include BOM driven DMU, real time BOM visualization, alignment automation for part-design lifecycle separation, and multiple views with partitions. Further user efficiencies are driven by active change and effectivity automation. All consumer users on a customer site must use the BOM consumer product that corresponds to its BOM authoring product.
Teamcenter 物料清单单元管理用于编写和高效管理复杂产品资产的单元工程物料清单，这些资产的物料清单由终端单元效率配置和跟踪。采用此类工程物料清单的典型行业包括航空与运输、运输和海事。Teamcenter BOM 单元管理在 Teamcenter 生态系统中实现互作性，提供流程效率和可扩展性，支持复杂产品的整个生命周期开发与维护。产品的亮点包括由物料清单驱动的DMU、实时物料清单可视化、用于零件设计生命周期分离的对齐自动化，以及带分区的多视图。积极变革和效率自动化进一步提升了用户效率。客户网站上的所有消费者用户必须使用对应其BOM创作产品的BOM消费产品。
