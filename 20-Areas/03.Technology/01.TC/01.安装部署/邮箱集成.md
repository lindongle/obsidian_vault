| **Preference Name** | **Preference Value** |
|----|----|
| Mail_server_charset | GBK |
| Mail_server_name | 填写“SMTP服务器的IP” |
| Mail_OSMail_activated | true |
| Mail_internal_mail_activated | true |
| Mail_server_port | “SMTP端口号”，一般默认25 |
| Mail_OS_from_address | 默认发件人，Exchange可不设置 |
