创建快照，部署会自动创建一个快照：/repository/snapshot对应的环境文件夹下
dc_quick_deploy -create_snapshot -dcurl=http://dc_host:8080/deploymentcenter -comment="test" -environment=Env_001 -dcusername=dcadmin -dcpassword=dcadmin
验证快照，列出所有快照
dc_quick_deploy -list_snapshot -dcurl=http://dc_host:8080/deploymentcenter -environment=Env_tc2412 -dcusername=dcadmin -dcpasswordfile="E:\admin passwords\dcadmin.pwf" -snapshot_file=c:\\snapshots.txt
![image1](ab784ba4d96f40698ca2738b5b41151b.png)
恢复快照：
dc_quick_deploy -apply_snapshot -snapshot=Env_tc2412_snapshot_2 -dcurl=http://dc_host:8080/deploymentcenter -environment=Env_tc2412 -dcusername=dcadmin -dcpasswordfile="E:\admin passwords\dcadmin.pwf"
