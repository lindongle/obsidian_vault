如何让提醒图标实时显示收到的消息提醒
2025年11月28日
9:50

The following preferences play a vital role here:
SCM_notification_mode=2 (1 - SMTP email,2 - Teamcenter inbox,3 - Both)


Subscription daemon sleeps for 15min ( OOTB) configured through a preference
TC_subscriptionmgrd_sleep_minutes=1
and the Polling interval is set to 5 min (OOTB) in AW configured via preference
AWS_Notifications_Polling_Interval=1


I set both to 1 min.

Then restarted the services
Teamcenter Action Manager Daemon
Teamcenter Subscription Manager Service
