Unable to view JT in AW 4.1 Symptom In this issue customer has Vis Server Manager connected to the assigner.
When he tries to view the JT in the AW viewer, he gets an error in the console as "All Default Viewer: no reference found for viewer dimension" "TypeError: Cannot read property "0" of undefined".
Hardware/Software Configuration Platform: INTL64 OS: WINDOWS OS Version: 2012_R2 Product: TEAMCENTER Application: ACTIVEWORKSPACE Version: V4.1 Function: 3D_VIEWER Solution Once the "Visualization Extension" is installed through "tem.bat the viewer started working.
Reference We verified that the visualization templates are not installed on the system and due to which the viewer is not working.
Once the "Visualization Extension" is installed through "tem.bat the viewer started working.
