After check the schedule related properties. two properties are not set properly.
Property 1: Published is set with false, correct it to true.
Property 2:Notification Enabled is set with false, correct it to true
Then notification email works normally

需要安装订阅管理器、任务监控管理器，并启动服务；
只对流程启动后有效；
