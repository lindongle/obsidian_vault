title: 根据数据集名称，查询数据集在物理卷文件中的路径
updated: 2026-06-05T23:32:46
created: 2026-07-05T17:04:47

select psd_path_name \|\| '\\ \|\| pfile_name from pimanfile ifile where
exists(select 'x' from pref_list_0 lst where lst.pvalu_0 = ifile.puid and
exists(select 'x' from pworkspaceobject obj where obj.puid = lst.puid and obj.pobject_name like '%ItemRevision%'))

将ItemRevision改成数据集名称
