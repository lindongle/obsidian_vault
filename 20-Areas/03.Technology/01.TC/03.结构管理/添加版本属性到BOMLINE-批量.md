**使用全局常数：** 
BOMLineFormConfiguredProperties  --«span style='font-family:微软雅黑;font-size:11.0pt'»Adds properties from item master types.«/span»

BOMLineRevConfiguredProperties  --«span style='font-family:微软雅黑;font-size:11.0pt'»Adds properties from item revision master types.«/span»

Additionally there are 2 more for Item and Item Revision:

Fnd0BOMLineItemConfigProps     --«span style='font-family:微软雅黑;font-size:11.0pt'»Adds properties from item types.«/span»

Fnd0BOMLineRevConfigProps      --«span style='font-family:微软雅黑;font-size:11.0pt'»Adds properties from item revision types.«/span»
