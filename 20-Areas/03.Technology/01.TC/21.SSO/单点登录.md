问题：部署11.3之后版本sso，启动weblogic提示错误。
2018-04-22 13:04:57,643 \[STANDBY\] ExecuteThread: '2' for queue: 'weblogic.kernel
.Default (self-tuning)' ERROR Null object returned for RollingFile in Appenders.
解决：
<span style='color:#1F497D'>This is a log42 error. In the TcSS Login Service or Identity Service \[war_file\]/WEB-INF/classes/log4j2.xml file. The following field causes the error:</span>
<span style='color:#1F497D'></span>
\<Property name="SERVER_FILE"\><span style='background:yellow;mso-highlight:yellow'>\${sys:CONTEXT_PATH}</span>.log\</Property\>
<span style='color:#1F497D'></span>
<span style='color:#1F497D'>This is a known log4j2 error that occurs in app servers during application start order. I don’t believe they have a solution yet. The work-around is the replace “\${sys:CONTEXT_PATH}” with a real filename, for example:</span>
<span style='color:#1F497D'></span>
\<Property name="SERVER_FILE"\><span style='background:yellow;mso-highlight:yellow'>LoginService</span>.log\</Property\>
<span style='color:#1F497D'>or</span>
\<Property name="SERVER_FILE"\><span style='background:yellow;mso-highlight:yellow'>IdentityService</span>.log\</Property\>
<span style='color:#333333'>修改完成后，weblogic中更新war包，并重启weblogic服务。</span>
