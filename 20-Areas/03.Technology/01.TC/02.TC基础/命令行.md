1、导入组织提示以下错误：
D:\Siemens\Teamcenter10\tc_menu\>plmxml_import -u=infodba -p=infodba -g=dba -xm
l_file=GroupMember.plmxml -import_mode=GroupMember

先查找log日志，默认在c:\temp文件夹中。根据提示：

Imported As ====\>
ERROR-Occurred with Message:

WARNING 515245 The number of active Author users exceeds the number of purchased licenses.
