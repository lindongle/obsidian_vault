nx初始化错误
2023年5月17日
9:47

Failure in XERCES_SAX_parse attemping to parse [file:FILE://C](file://FILE:/C): splm'nx90 ugii materials physicalmaterialdefinitions
Xml

解决：
\- Open "Control Panels"
\- Select the "Region and Language" Control Panel
\- Select the "Formats" pane
\- Change the Format to "English (United States)".
\- Close the Control Panel (a Reboot might be necessary)
\- Restart NX
