<span style='color:#2D373C'>**摘要**</span>
<span style='color:#2D373C'>Part Family Members are by default not to be exported from TC. Exporting an assembly containing such part might raise some questions. Two possibilities to export part family member is by either turning off both options in its dedicated chapter within Customer Defaults or by setting the environment variable “UGII_UGMGR_ALLOW_PFM_IMPORT_EXPORT=1”.</span>

<span style='color:#2D373C'>**详细信息**</span>
- <span style='font-family:Calibri; font-size:11.0pt;color:#2D373C'>Part Families Consist of Template Part (the mother ) and the Part Family Member (the child ). Part families are most often used to create library of standard parts e.g. whose dimensions are to be parametrized and varied. It is recommended to include template in the name of the mother , so this can be easily recognized e.g.</span>«span style='font-family:"Segoe UI";font-size:9.5pt;color:#212529;background:white'»bolt_M16\_«/span»«span style='font-weight:bold;font-family:"Segoe UI";font-size:9.5pt;color:#212529; background:white'»template«/span»«span style='font-family:"Segoe UI"; font-size:9.5pt;color:#212529;background:white'».prt . More information about Part Families can be found«/span»[«span style='font-family:"Segoe UI";font-size:9.5pt;background:white'»here«/span»](https://docs.sw.siemens.com/en-US/product/209349590/doc/PL20200605194735749.modeling/html/part_families_prtfam_ov)«span style='font-family:"Segoe UI";font-size:9.5pt;color:#212529;background:white'».«/span»
<span style='color:#2D373C'></span>
- <span style='color:#2D373C'>In Assembly Navigator through the column Info, we can quickly see overview of part family members (pls. see below). Currently we don t have such possibility within TC (rich client).</span>

![image1](2eb9642caa00473ca2327032e2e128f8.png)

<span style='color:#2D373C'></span>
- <span style='color:#2D373C'>Part Family Members are by default read only, it can only be modified through the template.</span>
<span style='color:#2D373C'></span>
- <span style='color:#2D373C'>The default settings in Customer Defaults do not allow Part Family Member to be exported.</span>
<span style='color:#2D373C'></span>
- <span style='color:#2D373C'>Default settings in Customer Defaults:</span>

![image2](0d6a922a220f43e3b6c5e55817593bd9.png)

<span style='color:#2D373C'></span>
- <span style='color:#2D373C'></span>
- <span style='color:#2D373C'>One way to allow export of Part Family Member is by turning off both options in Customer Defaults.</span>
<span style='color:#2D373C'>This will prompt a question when exporting the part member to have user decides either to treat the member as lost or to convert it as a normal part.</span>
<span style='color:#2D373C'>In the exported assembly, it will then be shown as a normal part (no indication it was used to be a part family member).</span>
<span style='color:#2D373C'>It will become modifiable.</span>

![image3](c9f1ea31bf8148f38e13e16f7e93e481.png)

![image4](574b493e8b334871ae72fb81682370c6.png)

<span style='color:#2D373C'></span>
- <span style='color:#2D373C'></span>
- <span style='font-family:Calibri; font-size:11.0pt;color:#2D373C'>Another possibility to export Part Family Member is to set the environment variable</span>«span style='font-weight:bold;font-family:"Segoe UI";font-size:9.5pt;color:#212529; background:white'»UGII_UGMGR_ALLOW_PFM_IMPORT_EXPORT=1«/span»«span style='font-family:"Segoe UI";font-size:9.5pt;color:#212529;background:white'».«/span»
<span style='background:white'>The difference to the previous method is that in the exported assembly, it will be exported as the original one. In assembly navigator part family member will be shown as such.</span>
<span style='background:white'>The part family member will also remain unmodifiable.</span>
<span style='background:white'>Setting this environment variable will take precedence and will overrule related settings in Customer Defaults.</span>
<span style='color:#212529;background:white'>More about export-import of part family member can be found</span>[<span style='background:white'>here</span>](https://docs.sw.siemens.com/en-US/product/209349590/doc/PL20191127135844554.tcint/html/clone_clone_impexp_part_fam?audience=external)<span style='color:#212529;background: white'>.</span>
