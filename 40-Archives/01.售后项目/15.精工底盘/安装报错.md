Tcserver process crashes while opening user's TaskInbox
Software Versions/Configuration:
=========================
Teamcenter 13.1.0.5
Description of Problem:
==================
After upgrading to TC13 the tcserver process crashes while expanding the content of user's TaskInbox.
The crash produces a core dump, see below the stack trace retrieved from the coredump file.
The OOTB preference TaskInbox_DefaultChildProperties contains by default three entries:
- contents
- fnd0ScheduleTasks
- fnd0SubscriptionMessage
By removing the entries "fnd0ScheduleTasks" and "fnd0SubscriptionMessage" from the preference TaskInbox_DefaultChildProperties, the tcserver process does not crash any longer and the TaskInbox correctly loads the TaskToPerform and TaskToTrack folders. By adding one of the additional entries "fnd0ScheduleTasks" or "fnd0SubscriptionMessage" into the preference makes the tcserver crash again and no content of the user's TakInbox is loaded (not even the TaskToPerform or TaskToTrack folders).
Steps to Duplicate:
==============
N/A. Only in customer environment.

========================
No
