## 领导力的进阶：责任不断加重

你的位置越高，需要承担的人和责任就越多。在一线岗位，成功的关键更多取决于执行力和专业能力；但当你进入管理与领导岗位时，这些能力便只是基本要求。真正区分人与人之间的，是能否承担更大的责任、处理更复杂的关系，并直面更高程度的不确定性。

The higher you rise, the more people and responsibilities you have to deal with. At the front-line level, success depends more on execution and professional skills. But when you move into management and leadership roles, those become only basic requirements. What truly differentiates people is whether they can handle greater responsibility, more complex relationships, and higher levels of uncertainty.

我认为，承担责任包含四个层次。每一层都比上一层更具挑战，也更加重要。

I believe the capacity to carry responsibility includes four levels of thinking. Each one is more challenging and more important than the last.



视频开篇：说明职位越高，需要承担的人与责任越多

## 第一层：承担自己与团队

第一层，是承担自己和团队，并在压力中完成任务。困难越大、挑战越复杂，就越考验领导者的能力。当项目出错、团队陷入焦虑时，你不能只是控制他们；面对批评和不公平对待，你也不能抱怨，而要保持镇定，理解当前处境并解决问题。这正是成为领导者的基础。

The first level is carrying yourself and your team through execution. The greater the pressure and the more complex the challenge you face when a project goes wrong and the team becomes anxious, the more this level is tested. You cannot simply control the team. When you face criticism and unfair treatment, you cannot complain; you must remain composed, understand the situation, and resolve the problem. This is the foundation of becoming a leader.



第一层责任：面对压力、批评与团队焦虑时保持镇定并解决问题

## 第二层：容纳差异与人性的复杂

第二层，是容纳他人，接纳不同的个性与观点。位置越高，看到的人性就越复杂。当利益被牵涉进来时，有人争抢功劳，有人逃避责任，有人屈从于诱惑，也有人为了个人利益改变立场。因为这些情况而愤怒、失望，或试图改变所有人，最终只会让自己精疲力竭。

The second level is challenging others and accepting different personalities and perspectives. The higher you rise, the more you see the complexity of human nature. When interests are involved, some people compete for credit, while others avoid responsibility, succumb to temptation, or change their position for personal gain. If you become angry, disappointed, or try to change everyone because of these situations, you will only exhaust yourself.

真正成熟的人，不会期待人性完美，也不会与人性对抗。他们能够承认现实，并在其中继续发挥领导作用。

Truly mature people neither expect human nature to be perfect nor fight against it. They accept reality and continue to lead within it.



第二层责任：接受不同观点、承认人性复杂并避免因他人而耗尽自己

## 第三层：承担复杂性与不确定性

第三层，是承担复杂性，在判断、不确定与冲突中作出决定。许多人认为，高级领导者最困难的部分是繁重的工作量；但真正的压力往往来自没有明确答案。信息永远不完整，意见永远无法完全一致，而每一次决策都伴随着风险。

The third level is carrying complexity, making judgments amid uncertainty and conflict. Many people think the hardest part of being a senior leader is the workload. But the real pressure often comes from having no clear answers. Information is never complete, opinions are never fully aligned, and decisions always come with risks.

当所有人都对方向感到不确定时，你不能只是等待，也不能把自己的焦虑传递给团队。你必须向前一步，明确方向、稳定节奏并作出决定，成为团队的锚点。保持头脑清醒，在不确定性中作出可靠判断，是高级领导者少有的稳定能力之一。

When everyone is uncertain about the direction, you cannot simply wait, and you cannot pass your anxiety to the team. You must step forward, set the direction, stabilize the pace, and make decisions. You become the anchor for the team. Staying clear-headed and making sound judgments amid uncertainty are among the rare stability skills of senior leaders.



第三层责任：在信息不完整、意见分歧和决策风险中成为团队的锚点

## 第四层：承担使命并创造长期价值

第四层，也是最难培养的一层，最能体现领导者的愿景：承担使命，并对更大的目标负责。这一阶段，领导者最大的变化，是不再只把自己视为管理者，而把自己视为价值的创造者。

The final level is the hardest to develop and best reflects a leader's vision: carrying a mission and taking responsibility for a greater purpose. At this stage, the biggest change for a leader is no longer seeing themselves as just a manager, but as a creator of value.

真正的领导者不再只关注眼前的目标和结果，而会追问：这个组织的目的是什么？我想把这些人带向哪里？我希望创造怎样的长期价值？领导层级越高，就越不能只解决今天的问题，而必须为未来确立方向。他们所承担的，不仅是业务指标，更是帮助组织成长、让更多人取得成功的责任。

True leaders no longer focus only on immediate goals and results. They ask: What is the purpose of this organization? Where do I want to lead these people? What long-term value do I want to create? The higher the level of leadership, the less they can focus only on solving today's problems; they must create direction for the future. What they carry is not just business targets, but the responsibility to help the organization grow and enable more people to achieve success.

## 核心总结

领导力的成长，本质上是责任范围的持续扩大：先承担自己与团队，再容纳他人的差异；继而承担复杂性和不确定性，最终承担使命并创造长期价值。位置越高，越需要依靠判断、承担与愿景，而不是仅仅依靠技能和执行。

The growth of leadership is, in essence, the continual expansion of one's capacity for responsibility: first carrying yourself and your team, then accepting the differences between others; next carrying complexity and uncertainty; and finally carrying a mission and creating long-term value. The higher your position, the more you must rely on judgment, responsibility, and vision rather than on skills and execution alone.
